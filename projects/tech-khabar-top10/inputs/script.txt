Namaste doston, swagat hai aapka Tech Khabar mein, jahan milti hain tech ki sabse badi khabrein, seedhi aur simple Hinglish mein.
Aaj ki video mein hain poori das badi khabrein, toh video ko end tak zaroor dekhna.
Number one, Apple ne apna pehla touchscreen MacBook Pro launch kar diya hai. Same chip wale normal MacBook Pro se ye lagbhag 400 dollar mehnga hai, aur deliveries 6 November se shuru ho chuki hain.
Number two, GTA6 ke fans ke liye bohot badi khabar. 10 November se aap game preload kar sakte ho. Game ka size hai 212 GB, toh storage abhi se khali kar lo. Launch hai 19 November ko.
Number three, AMD ne 3 November ko apne third quarter ke official results jaari kiye.
Number four, China ne SoftBank ka ek bada deal rok diya hai. SoftBank ABB ka robotics business kharidna chahta tha. ABB ne 5 November ko iss se confirm kiya, lekin reports mein alag alag bataaya gaya hai ki rokne wali body kaunsi thi.
Number five, Blue Origin ka New Glenn rocket ab dobara udaan January 2027 tak nahi bharega. Reuters ke mutabik ye baat ek internal memo mein likhi gayi hai.
Number six, AI se nakal ka fraud. America mein teen logon par charges lage hain ki unhon ne AI se CEOs aur vakilon ki nakal karke lagbhag 139 million dollar churai. Dhyan rahe, abhi sirf charges lage hain, kuch sabit nahi hua hai.
Number seven, Japan ki furniture company Nitori ka data breach hua hai, jisme saadhe 37 million tak customer records chori hue hai. Company ke mutabik passwords aur card details surakshit hain.
Number eight, Paris mein 9 November ko AI safety par ek meeting hui, Paris Call for AI Safety, jisme lagbhag 50 participants thi. Ye voluntary hai, yani kanuni tor par kisi ko baandhata nahi.
Number nine, Google ka naya model, Gemini 4 Carbon, 10 November ko sab ke liye aa gaya hai. Pricing ke liye Google ka official page zarur check karna.
Aur number ten, Anthropic ne 9 November ko Fable 5.2 aur Mythos 5.2 launch kiye. Company ke mutabik ye pichle 5.1 se 30% tak tez hain.
Toh dosto, ye thi aaj ki 10 badi tech khabrein. Aapko inme se kaunsi khabar sabse zyada pasand aayi, comment mein zarur batana.
Aur agar GTA6 ke liye aap excited ho, to comment mein likho GTA. Agar video pasand aayi ho to like zarur karo, apne dosto ke saath share karo, aur Tech Khabar channel ko subscribe karna mat bhoolna. Saath mein bell icon bhi dabana, taaki har nayi khabar sabse pehle aap tak pahunche. Milte hain agli video mein. Tab tak ke liye jude rahiye Tech Khabar ke saath. Jai Hind.
