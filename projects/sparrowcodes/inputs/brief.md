## Benefits worth saying:

- No auto-renew. Plans last 30 days. Buying again adds 30 more days on top.

- No surprise spend. When you hit a limit, you choose whether to keep going on credits.

- Sparrow Auto picks a fast, low-cost model for easy tasks and a stronger one for hard tasks. In SparrowCodes' own test of ten coding tasks, it passed everything Claude Sonnet 5.5 passed for 92% less.

- Pay as you go if you don't want a plan: prepaid credits from \$5. Videos are \$0.25 per 15 seconds with no watermark.

- Refer a developer: you get 10% of their first paid top-up as credits, up to \$10.

Prices are before tax; customers in India pay 18% GST and can pay in rupees with UPI. Source: Pricing, Teams. [URL 🔗](https://sparrowcodes.com/pricing)

## Dos, don'ts and links

Stick to the numbers in this brief, and show real screens rather than mock-ups.

## Do

- Use Instagram's Paid partnership label and add #ad if this is a paid post.

- Say "10 videos every 30 days" and "from \$10 for 30 days", exactly as the site does.

- Say Video is in Beta, and that the free videos carry a small mark.

- Mention that the free model may keep request data, if you show the free coding agent.

## Don't

- Don't say "unlimited". Every plan has usage limits that reset every 5 hours and every week.

- Don't say the Free plan includes Claude Sonnet or Opus. Only Pro and Team Pro include every model.

- Don't call the music "licensed" or "royalty-free". Say "music picked to match the mood".

- Don't promise an exact render time. Say "a few minutes".

- Don't show API keys or account emails on screen.
